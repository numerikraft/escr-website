Personality
ES-CR's brand personality balances the precision of clinical science with the human warmth of a mission-driven organization. These four traits guide every touchpoint — from sponsor presentations to patient-facing materials.

Color Usage Rules
When to Use What
Indigo Primary
Primary action buttons, key headlines, active states, section dividers. Minimum 40% of brand-color presence.
Violet
Secondary CTAs, graph/chart accents, hover states. Maintains purple-family cohesion.
Acid Green — Accent Only
Use sparingly — maximum 10% of color surface on any design. Highlights, badges, progress indicators, notifications.
Ink + Fog
Default backgrounds (Fog) and text (Ink). Always maintain a 4.5:1 contrast ratio minimum.
Digital hex values shown. CMYK reference on slide 11. Pantone matching available in print asset kit.

Editorial Philosophy
Brand
Voice
"We communicate with the precision of a protocol and the clarity of a colleague."
Every word we publish represents the scientific rigor and human care at the core of clinical research. We are authoritative without being cold, precise without being pedantic.

Tone by Context Matrix
When & How
Sponsor RFP
Confident, technical, structured. Lead with data, follow with context.
Site PI Meeting
Collaborative, clear. Acknowledge complexity, offer solutions.
Patient ICF
Plain language, empathetic. Grade 8 reading level maximum.
Regulator Dossier
Formal, evidence-first. Passive voice permitted where standard.
LinkedIn / PR
Mission-forward, optimistic. Avoid jargon. Highlight human impact.
