The Brand

Founded in 2023, ES-CR specializes in clinical research in Algeria, combining local expertise with international standards. Driven by a multidisciplinary team combining expertise in clinical research, pharmacoeconomic studies, medical affairs, academic teaching and research, we place quality, scientific rigor and operational excellence at the heart of everything we do.

Logo

The corporate mark is a tripartite system composed of the brand acronym, an interlocking dual-concentric hexagon symbol, and the institutional category descriptor.

Logo Type & Architecture

The master lockup is constructed around three structural elements: the central dual-concentric hexagon symbol acting as an optical focal point, flanked by the "ES" and "CR" logotype custom-set in the geometric display font Saved by Zero, and anchored below by the baseline descriptor "Contract Research Organization" set in CDR 플라워 (CDR Flower).

Clear Space & Exclusion Zone

The exclusion zone defines the mandatory perimeter around the master lockup that must remain clear of all typography, graphics, margins, and trim boundaries. The isolation unit X is calibrated directly from the width of the central hexagon mark (X = 39.15 pt), guaranteeing proportional optical protection across all applications.

Minimum Reproduction Limits

To guarantee optical legibility and line weight retention across varied production media, the master corporate lockup is constrained by verified reproduction thresholds: a minimum width of 40.00 mm (height: 10.15 mm, 151 px @ 96 dpi) for offset and digital print, and 180 px (height: 45.69 px @1x) for digital screens, applications, and presentation decks. Below these limits, the full lockup must not be reduced; the standalone 02 Brandmark must be deployed instead.

Brandmark

The standalone dual-concentric hexagon symbol functions as the independent brandmark, engineered for browser favicons, application icons, and compact contexts where the full horizontal logotype cannot be deployed.

Mark Grid & Construction

The brandmark geometry is rooted in a 30° isometric molecular grid with verified vector coordinates. Two interlocking regular hexagons represent the core CRO nexus: the investigator trial site and the biopharma sponsor, bonded by shared clinical integrity.

Minimum Reproduction Limits

Engineered for extreme compact rendering, the standalone brandmark maintains geometric fidelity and contrast across physical and digital micro-formats, establishing strict minimum reproduction thresholds.

Typography Do’s & Don’ts

Adhere strictly to font pairings, leading standards, and hierarchy rules to preserve legibility and scientific authority.

DO — Approved Typography Standards
DON'T — Unapproved Typography Violations
Akzidenz Title + Libre Body

Clear typographic contrast between authoritative headline grotesk and comfortable, readable body copy.

Approved Font Pairing
Unapproved Novelty & Scripts

Mixing decorative scripts and unrelated serif typefaces degrades institutional credibility.

Unauthorized Typefaces
Comfortable Leading (1.65)

Extended clinical text requires calibrated line height to facilitate effortless protocol review without eye fatigue.

Optimal Line Spacing
Cramped Leading (< 1.1)

Tight vertical line height causes ascending and descending glyphs to collide, causing eye strain and reading errors.

Collapsed Leading
